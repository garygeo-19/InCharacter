## Characters react to what the learner said (Coach first)

**Seen:** in the Coach session, Alex's next line doesn't reflect what the learner just shared (owner test, 1 Nov 2026).
Every line is written, checked and recorded ahead of time, and Muse Spark only chooses which one comes next, so Alex
can't pick up the learner's specifics.

**Plan (owner, 1 Nov 2026):** grow the reactions from real use. Collect the kinds of replies learners actually give at
each turn, cluster them, then write, check and record a short reaction for each common kind, so Spark can pick a
fitting reaction as well as the next line. Over time the characters are prepared for most replies while staying
grounded in the notes and pre-voiced.

**Before collecting anything:** the app currently promises "What you say is sent to Meta's API to be transcribed and
graded. In Character doesn't keep it." Collecting replies needs an explicit opt-in, an updated privacy line, and
anonymous storage.

**Considered for now and set aside:** a live one-sentence reaction written by Spark and spoken with ElevenLabs on the
server (more responsive, but not pre-checked, and it adds a server-side ElevenLabs key and about a second per turn);
the same reaction as text only.
